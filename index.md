# International Logic Friends

A student-run (virtual) logic community that hosts reading groups and discussion.

We welcome anyone, no matter specific interest or background.

## Current reading group(s)

As of summer 2026, we are running the __ILF Categorical Logic__ reading group. We meet on Tuesdays at 8 PM eastern on our discord server to discuss **Topoi: The Categorial Analysis of Logic** by Robert Goldblatt. We would love to have you join us!
