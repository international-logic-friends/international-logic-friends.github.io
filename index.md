# International Logic Friends

{: .lede}
A student-run virtual logic community that hosts reading groups and discussion.

We welcome anyone, no matter specific interest or background.

## Current reading group(s)

As of summer 2026, we are running the **ILF Categorical Logic** reading group. We meet on Tuesdays at 8 PM Eastern on our Discord server to discuss **Topoi: The Categorical Analysis of Logic** by Robert Goldblatt. We would love to have you join us!

## Join

The Discord is public to join. Simply apply through [this](https://discord.com/invite/GqyMYurz3) by providing your name, institution if you have one, and education level. All are welcome, but this provides context to the moderators. Applications are in place to prevent bots and spam, not to prevent people from joining.
