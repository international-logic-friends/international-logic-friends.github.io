## About

**International Logic Friends** was created to foster collaboration and build friendships in logic amongst students at different universities around the world. 

Currently, we have members from five universities and two countries, so we are technically international!

The group is run by **Randall Rickel**, a PhD student in the mathematics department at the **University of North Texas**, and **Mohith Muddasani** at **The Ohio State University**.
